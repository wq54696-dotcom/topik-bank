Questions 34-39 in TOPIK I Reading give you two short sentences with one blank. The blank can be a particle (`을/를`, `에`, `에서`), a verb, an adverb or a noun. These are some of the easiest points on the test, as long as you check three things.

## What the question looks like

The first sentence sets up a situation. The second sentence has a blank. You choose the word that fits **both** the meaning of the situation and the grammar of the sentence.

## 3-step check

1. **Read the situation first.** "It's cold", "The bus isn't coming", "It's my friend's birthday". The answer has to make sense in that situation.
2. **Check the grammar around the blank.**
   - Particles: does the noun end in a vowel or a consonant? (`사과를`, `책을`)
   - Verb pairs: Korean uses different verbs for different clothes: `입다` (coat, shirt), `쓰다` (hat), `신다` (shoes).
   - Giving and receiving: `친구에게 주다` vs `친구에게서 받다`.
3. **Read the full sentence with your answer.** If it sounds like something a person would actually say, you're done.

## Practice

> (&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;)에 들어갈 가장 알맞은 것을 고르십시오.

**Q1.** 날씨가 춥습니다. (&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;) 입습니다.

① 모자를 ② 코트를 ③ 우산을 ④ 구두를

**Q2.** 내일은 친구 생일입니다. 친구에게 선물을 (&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;).

① 받습니다 ② 줍니다 ③ 팝니다 ④ 찾습니다

**Q3.** 저는 과일을 좋아합니다. 사과(&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;) 자주 먹습니다.

① 를 ② 을 ③ 에서 ④ 이

**Q4.** 버스가 오지 않습니다. 그래서 학교에 (&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;) 갑니다.

① 걸어서 ② 자주 ③ 같이 ④ 아까

<details>
<summary><strong>Show answers</strong></summary>

<p><strong>Q1: ②</strong> The verb is <code>입다</code>, which goes with a coat. A hat uses <code>쓰다</code>, shoes use <code>신다</code>, and you don't "wear" an umbrella.</p>

<p><strong>Q2: ②</strong> <code>친구에게</code> + birthday tells you that you are <em>giving</em> the present. To receive from a friend, Korean says <code>친구에게서 받다</code>.</p>

<p><strong>Q3: ①</strong> You need an object particle. <code>사과</code> ends in a vowel, so it takes <code>를</code>, not <code>을</code>.</p>

<p><strong>Q4: ①</strong> <code>그래서</code> means the second sentence is a result of the first. The bus isn't coming, so you go to school <em>on foot</em> (<code>걸어서</code>).</p>

</details>
