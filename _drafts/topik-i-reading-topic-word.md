TOPIK I Reading starts at question 31 with three quick questions: you read two very short sentences and choose the **one word** that describes what they are about. These are free points if you know the trick.

## What the question looks like

Two sentences, usually under ten words each, about the same thing. The four choices are general nouns such as `계절`, `직업`, `가족`, `음식`, `날씨`, `취미`.

## The umbrella-word trick

1. **Find the matching words in both sentences.** In "여름에는 바다에 갑니다. 겨울에는 스키를 탑니다," the words that pair up are `여름` and `겨울`.
2. **Ask: what group do they both belong to?** Summer and winter are both seasons (`계절`). That's your umbrella word.
3. **Watch out for the trap.** One wrong choice often matches only *one* word in the sentences. `스키` is a sport, so `운동` looks tempting, but it doesn't cover the first sentence.

## Words to know

| Umbrella word | Covers |
| --- | --- |
| 계절 | 봄, 여름, 가을, 겨울 |
| 직업 | 회사원, 학생, 의사, 선생님 |
| 가족 | 아버지, 어머니, 형, 언니, 동생 |
| 음식 | 빵, 밥, 김치, 라면 |
| 날씨 | 덥다, 춥다, 비가 오다, 맑다 |
| 취미 | 운동, 그림, 노래, 여행 |
| 장소 | 학교, 공원, 시장, 병원 |
