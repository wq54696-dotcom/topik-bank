TOPIK II is one test for four levels. Your total score decides whether you get **Level 3, 4, 5 or 6**. The questions get harder as the numbers go up, and each number range always tests the same skill. That means you can find your weak types and train them one by one.

## The test at a glance

| Section | Questions | Time | Points |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Listening | 1-50 (50 questions) | 60 minutes | 100 |
| Writing | 51-54 (4 questions) | 50 minutes | 100 |
| Reading | 1-50 (50 questions) | 70 minutes | 100 |
| **Total** | **104 questions** | **180 minutes** | **300** |

- **Level 3:** 120 points or more
- **Level 4:** 150 points or more
- **Level 5:** 190 points or more
- **Level 6:** 230 points or more

Listening and Writing are taken together in the first period, and Reading in the second. This is the paper-based test (PBT) format. The internet-based test (IBT) is organized differently. Check [topik.go.kr](https://www.topik.go.kr) for the current format before you register.

## Where to start

- **Aiming for Level 3-4:** Start with the lower-numbered reading types and Writing 51-53. They use shorter texts and more everyday topics.
- **Aiming for Level 5-6:** Add the higher-numbered reading types and the Writing 54 essay, which is worth half of the writing score.
