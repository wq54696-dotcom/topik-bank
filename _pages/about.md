## How the questions are made

- Every passage, dialogue and answer choice is written from scratch.
- Questions follow the official TOPIK question types and difficulty levels.
- Each answer comes with a short English explanation so you can learn from mistakes.

This site is not affiliated with the National Institute for International Education (NIIED), which administers TOPIK. For official test information, visit [topik.go.kr](https://www.topik.go.kr).
