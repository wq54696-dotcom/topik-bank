TOPIK I is the beginner test. Your total score decides whether you get **Level 1** or **Level 2**. The question types appear in the same order every time, so once you know what each number asks, you can practice one type at a time.

## The test at a glance

| Section | Questions | Time | Points |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Listening | 1-30 (30 questions) | 40 minutes | 100 |
| Reading | 31-70 (40 questions) | 60 minutes | 100 |
| **Total** | **70 questions** | **100 minutes** | **200** |

- **Level 1:** 80 points or more
- **Level 2:** 140 points or more
- All questions are multiple choice with four options. There is no writing section.

This is the paper-based test (PBT) format. The internet-based test (IBT) has fewer questions and a shorter time. Check [topik.go.kr](https://www.topik.go.kr) for the current format before you register.

2. Find those numbers in the table and read the guide for that type.
3. Practice that one type until the method feels automatic, then move to the next.
