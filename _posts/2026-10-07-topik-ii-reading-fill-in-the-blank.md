Fill-in-the-blank questions ask you to choose the phrase that best completes a short passage. They look easy, but the wrong choices are designed to be *almost* right.

## What the question looks like

You read a short passage (2-4 sentences) with one blank and choose the best answer from four options. The key is in the sentences **around** the blank, not in the blank itself.

## 3-step method

1. **Find the reason clue.** Look for connectors such as `-아/어서`, `-기 때문에`, `그러니까`. They tell you what the blank must follow from.
2. **Predict before you look.** Say the answer in your own words first, then find the option closest to it.
3. **Check the logic.** Read the full sentence with your choice. If it doesn't connect to the clue, it's wrong even if the grammar is fine.
